# Reuse stats

Trail `f1943ab0`. The CIP-01 to CIP-05 rewrite in NIP style runs apart, on `feat-spec-nip` (trail `bf25025a`).

## Goal

A simple write surface and a rich report. Agents write one new flag, `--uses`, and use `--replaces` on a
done step. The reader derives follows, reuse, savings and revisions from the records.

## Phase 1: prompts replace steers

- The prompt hook writes a `prompt` mark on the trail only. A reader ignores old `steer` records.
- A grade is `took` and `revised`: the steps that replace the step after its done. No `steered`, no turns.
- Trail states: active, reached, revised, dropped, expired.
- A review's `asked` holds the prompts on the trail while the step was open.

Verify: `make verify`; tests change first.

## Phase 2: the write surface

- `step add --uses <id>...`: the id of a live decision, or of a step of another trail. A trail id resolves
  to its first ask. Unknown: `no-step`. Same trail: a usage error. On a trail and its ask, the tags go on
  the ask only.
- `step add --replaces <id>` also takes a done step of any trail. It stays done and reads as revised.
- CIP-06 in NIP style: the tags, their meaning, what a reader derives.

Verify: `tests/reuse.rs`, `make verify`.

## Phase 3: the report

- `Reuse`: the follow graph, the walk up and down at any depth, use counts, revisions.
- A saving is the source's `took` less the follower's, shown as is below zero.
- `celis stats`: followed steps, most reached first, with saved and revised; used decisions; the total.
- `celis stats <id>`: the step, its sources, then its followers, depth first.
- `tree --full`: `follows`, `reused` and `revised` lines; `tree --json`: `reused`.

Verify: tests, `make verify`.

## Phase 4: the plugin

- The card's `step add` line gets `--uses`, and one sentence on `--uses` and on `--replaces` a done step.
- `past.md` copies each plan step's id, and prefers work that was not revised.

Verify: `tests/help.rs`, `tests/plugin.rs`.

## Order

Phase 1 stands alone. Phases 3 and 4 need Phase 2. The spec rewrite merges after Phase 1; spec conflicts
take the rewrite's shape with this trail's meaning.

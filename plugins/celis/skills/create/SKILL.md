---
name: create
description: Does a creative ask, such as an image, a logo, a sound, a level or a text, in layers with variants, so the person can pick by taste, after the work or at each layer. Use when an ask makes something whose quality is a matter of taste, or when the person asks to choose or steer at each layer.
---

Creative work has no check that proves it good: the person's taste does. Do the ask as the card says,
in layers, with these changes:

1. Before the outer layer, take the patterns that bear on the ask, as the card's Recall says, and open
   the files of their examples, to see what the person chose before.
2. The outer layer has 2 variants as steps under it, unless the person names another number: one
   follows the patterns and the past picks, one explores away from them and names the pattern it
   breaks. Close each with `--file` for its result, so that its blob stays when the next variant writes
   the same file; when the last one closes, celis prints the blobs beside it.
3. At each choice point of each layer, such as a colour, a layout or a tone, weigh the forces of the
   patterns that bear on it, choose like their good examples, and name the pattern in the step's proof.
4. When the person asks to steer, even a small ask: after each layer, show them its result, and wait
   for `go` or notes before the next. At the outer layer, open the variants' blobs side by side, say in
   one line how they differ, and wait for the person's pick. A note, such as "move it left", is not a
   fix of done work: do it in the next layer. When they want to steer every ask, write it as a `me`
   pattern.
5. Else go as far as you can: choose the variant closer to the good examples, name it in the next
   layer's title, and finish. Only the outer layer has variants, unless the person asks.
6. In the end, name your choice on the "I chose" line with the pattern that decided it, and the blob of
   each other variant, so the person can pick after. Keep each pick, during the work or after it, as a
   pattern (`steps.md`, "Patterns"), in the person's words, and redo the next layers from it.

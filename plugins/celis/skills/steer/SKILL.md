---
name: steer
description: Does one ask layer by layer with the person, who picks a variant and gives notes at each layer before the next. Use when the person runs /celis:steer <ask>, or asks to choose or steer at each step of a creative or larger ask.
---

The text after `/celis:steer` is the ask. Do it as the card says, with these changes:

1. Split it by layers, as the card says, even when it is small.
2. When a layer closes, show the person its result, and wait for `go` or notes before the next layer.
   A note, such as "move it left", is not a fix of done work: do it in the next layer.
3. At the outer layer of creative work, make the variants as `steps.md`, "Creative work", says, open
   their blobs side by side, say in one line how they differ, and wait for the person's pick. Keep the
   pick as a pair, in the person's words, and give the next layer `--uses <pick>`.
4. When the person wants to steer every ask like this, write it as a `me` rule.
